UAMSWP News Syndication shows news posts from another WordPress site, from this site, or from both, through one shortcode: `[uamswp_news]`. Editors place it on a page or post and choose how the list looks; developers can change what it requests and how it renders through filters.

This wiki holds the documentation: a walkthrough, task guides, the reference and troubleshooting. The [README](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/UAMSWP-News-Syndication-2020#readme) keeps an overview, the requirements, installation and the development notes.
