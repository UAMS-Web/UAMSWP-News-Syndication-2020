## The wiki

These pages live in [`docs/wiki/`](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/UAMSWP-News-Syndication-2020/tree/master/docs/wiki) and are published by the **Publish wiki** workflow, on each release and when it is started by hand. A publish replaces the whole wiki with that folder, so a change goes through a pull request; an edit made here in the browser is lost at the next publish.
